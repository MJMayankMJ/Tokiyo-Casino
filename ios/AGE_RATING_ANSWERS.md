## Resulting expected rating

Selecting **Frequent/Intense Simulated Gambling** typically results in a **17+** rating regardless of other answers, because Apple bumps any app with simulated gambling into the highest age tier. That's expected and **not a rejection** — it just sets the App Store age gate.

## Things to align with the listing

- **App Store description** must include: "For entertainment only. No real money. Coins have no cash value."
- **Screenshots** must not imply real-money prizes, cash payouts, or purchasable currency.
- **Category** should be **Games → Casino** or **Games → Card** (Card is safer if you want to avoid the "Casino" sub-category flag).
- **Privacy** answers in App Store Connect should reflect: no tracking, no data collection, no data linked to user, no third-party SDKs.

## Reminder

Apple guideline 5.3.4 specifically targets **real-money gambling**. This app is virtual-only, so 5.3.4 does **not** apply, but reviewers may still scrutinize casino-themed UX. The disclaimer copy, age rating answers, and metadata above are designed to make that scrutiny resolve quickly.
