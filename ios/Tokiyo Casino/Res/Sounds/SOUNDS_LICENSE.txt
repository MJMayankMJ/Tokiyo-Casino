Table sounds in this folder (tk_*.caf)

Card, chip and knock sounds are trimmed, level-matched edits of recordings
from Kenney's "Casino Audio" and "Impact Sounds" packs
(https://kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

tk_slam_* layer an "Impact Sounds" soft impact under a "Casino Audio"
card placement.
