## Description (4000 char max)
```
Tokiyo Cards is a friendly, free-to-play home for classic card games. Pull up a seat, stack your chips, and play Texas Hold'em poker solo against AI opponents or with friends sitting right next to you.

No real money. No accounts. No internet required for local play. Just cards.

— TEXAS HOLD'EM POKER —
Play full Texas Hold'em hands with clean, readable tables and smooth betting controls. Choose your table size, pick your AI difficulty from Easy to Expert, and set your starting chip stack. Every hand is dealt fairly with proper poker rules — blinds, raises, folds, all-in, and showdown.

— PLAY WITH FRIENDS NEARBY —
Host a table and let friends in the same room join instantly over a direct device-to-device connection. No Wi-Fi, no servers, no sign-up — just open the app, create a table, and play together. Perfect for trips, flights, and anywhere the internet won't reach.

— SOLO AGAINST AI —
Not near other players? Sharpen your game against AI opponents with distinct playing styles. Adjust the difficulty to match your skill and learn the ropes at your own pace.

— VIRTUAL CHIPS, ZERO PRESSURE —
Every chip in Tokiyo Cards is virtual and exists only for fun. There are no in-app purchases, no real-money betting, and nothing to cash out. Start with a welcome stack and just enjoy the game.

— MORE GAMES ON THE WAY —
Tokiyo Cards is growing. Jackaroo is coming soon, with more table favorites to follow.

For entertainment only. Virtual chips have no real-world value.
```
